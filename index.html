What are MUNs, and what is AISMN

From a conference seat to a national network.

What are MUNs?

Model United Nations is an educational simulation of the United Nations and other international decision-making bodies. Students represent countries, diplomats or stakeholders, research global affairs, draft resolutions, deliver speeches and negotiate solutions with other delegates.

  • Public speaking
  • Diplomacy
  • Research
  • Critical thinking
  • Leadership
The opportunity. MUNs already teach students how to debate and negotiate. AISMN takes the experience further by helping students turn discussions into collaboration, practical skills and real-world initiatives.

Every MUN can become the beginning of something bigger.

Problem statement

Two gaps that MUNs leave open.

Problem 01

Regional divide and lack of awareness

  • Limited awareness of MUNs among students across India.
  • A disconnect between North and South India restricts collaboration and equal access to opportunities.
Problem 02

The post-MUN gap and lack of practical skills

  • Delegate connections often disappear after conferences, preventing long-term collaboration.
  • Overemphasis on foreign affairs and debate limits chances to build practical skills, projects and entrepreneurship.
Our innovative solutions
Solution 01

One Nation, One Student Network

  • Connect students from North India, South India and other regions through one national platform.
  • Collaborate with schools, colleges and MUN communities across India.
  • Promote cultural exchange, equal access and cross-regional teamwork.
Solution 02

Skill-based committees and interdisciplinary innovation

  • Committees led by skilled individuals across coding, art, music, entrepreneurship, finance and other fields.
  • Students combine diverse skills, exchange ideas and solve real-world problems.
  • Committee discussions become practical projects, innovative ideas and potential startups.
Solution 03

Beyond MUN: a continuous innovation ecosystem

  • Year-round workshops, competitions, interactive sessions and networking events.
  • Lasting connections between delegates, mentors and skilled individuals beyond conferences.
  • Collaboration with promising student innovators to develop projects and support startup building.

How we make successful collaborations

Bridging regions, one connection at a time.

AISMN aims to bridge regional divides and create a unified student network across India.

  • Cross-regional partnershipsConnect schools, colleges and MUN societies from North and South India.
  • Joint conferencesBring delegates from different states together through online and hybrid events.
  • Regional ambassadorsDevelop student-led representation across participating states.
  • Cultural exchangeUnderstand different languages, cultures, perspectives and regional challenges.
  • Mixed-state teamsPair students from different regions for research, debates and innovation projects.
  • Shared opportunitiesMake mentorship, workshops, competitions and networking accessible across geographical boundaries.

Our principle: Geography should never limit a student's access to opportunity.
